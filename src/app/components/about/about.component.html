About Us

Congrats Grads is an online library that showcases seniors and their accomplishments. As our goal is to honor seniors during these uncertain times, it is completely free! Congrats Grads also has an Instagram and Facebook page that has senior shoutouts, contests, and giveaways.

Congrats Grads all started back in April 2020 when COVID-19 struck the US. Alyssa, a high school senior, had to forego the last three months of her senior year and miss out on prom, her senior trip, and a "normal" graduation. Since many other seniors were struggling as well, Richie and Alyssa decided to create this website to honor the class of 2020. Congrats Grads showcased over 200 seniors in the first year and hopes to continue spreading this joy to seniors for many years to come.
